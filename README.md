A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857) bounty entry: one program, one job — write a single hilarious line of at most 200 characters that lands entirely on its own.

## The entry

`main.py` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My grandfather left me his watch, his war stories, and a restraining order against a goose he swore was a government drone.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

No input, no network, no dependencies — just Python 3.
